Replacement Window FAQs

Questions Knoxville homeowners ask

Need an answer about your specific windows? Send us a photo or request an estimate.

How do I know whether to repair or replace my windows?

A single broken pane, screen or hardware issue may be repairable. Windows with ongoing drafts, difficult operation, widespread deterioration or multiple failures may be better candidates for replacement.

Do you replace just a few windows?

Yes. Replacement projects can range from individual problem windows to larger whole-home projects depending on your needs.

Do new windows help with energy efficiency?

Modern insulated replacement windows can reduce heat transfer compared with many older windows. Actual savings depend on the home, existing windows, installation and other factors.

How much do replacement windows cost in Knoxville?

Pricing varies by size, style, glass package, quantity and installation conditions. Cunningham Windows provides free estimates so homeowners can get project-specific pricing.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Knoxville, Corryton and surrounding East Tennessee communities. Contact us to confirm service for your location.
