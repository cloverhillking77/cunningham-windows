Window Repair FAQs

Common questions

Can a fogged window be repaired?

Fogging between panes often indicates a failed insulated glass seal. Depending on the window, replacing the glass unit may be an option without replacing the entire window.

Do you replace broken window glass?

Yes. Cunningham Windows provides glass replacement for many residential window and door applications.
